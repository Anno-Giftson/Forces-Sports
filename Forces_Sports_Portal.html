DIAA Physicals & Eligibility

All Charter School of Wilmington student-athletes must submit an approved DIAA Pre-Participation Physical Evaluation (PPE) dated on or after April 1 prior to tryouts, alongside academic eligibility verification.

1. DIAA Physical Form

Signed by a licensed physician, nurse practitioner, or PA-C and uploaded prior to first practice.

2. Academic Standing

Students must pass a minimum of five credits (including two major subjects) per marking period.
